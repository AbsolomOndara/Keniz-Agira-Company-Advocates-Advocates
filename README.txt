KENIZ AGIRA & COMPANY ADVOCATES WEBSITE

This package contains the complete 29-page static website.

START HERE
Open index.html in a browser. Keep the html files and the css, js and images
folders together so that all links and assets continue to work.

STRUCTURE
index.html and about.html retain their custom page compositions.
The remaining pages use the shared css/ka-site.css and js/ka-site.js files.
The source uses local image files in the images folder.

NAMING
Homepage selectors use ka-home-.
About overview selectors use ka-about-.
Shared site components use ka-site-.
Every page has its own unique main identifier based on the page filename.

CONTENT APPROVAL
The wider legal service list is marked as subject to confirmation.
Legal team and forensic partner pages are intentionally unpublished.
The founder page does not invent qualifications or use a fictional portrait.
The legal notices are clearly identified as drafts for review by the firm.
The consultation form is a design-ready front end and does not transmit data
until an approved secure receiving service is connected.

IMAGERY
The editorial images are representative generated images featuring African
professionals and Nairobi settings. They do not depict actual employees,
clients, partners or premises of the firm. Replace them with approved firm
photography when available.

FOOTER
Every generated page includes the Worldways Web Developers credit and an
automatically updating copyright year.
