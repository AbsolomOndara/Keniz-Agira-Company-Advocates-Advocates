About the firm

Built for the legal questions of today.

Keniz Agira & Company Advocates is a Nairobi-based law firm created to make the relationship between law and technology easier to understand and act upon.

Our firm

A legal practice shaped by where the world is going.

The firm was established to bridge a widening gap between legal practice and the technology that now influences business, evidence, communication and daily life.

Our focus on technology does not remove the human side of legal work. Every matter still begins with a person, an organisation or a family that needs to understand its position and decide what to do next.

We therefore combine current legal thinking with careful listening, clear communication and respect for the individual circumstances behind every instruction.

KA Law and Tech Simplified.

Our founder

Founded by Keniz Agira with a clear purpose.

Keniz Agira established the firm to create a place where clients can seek thoughtful legal guidance on matters involving both established law and emerging technology.

The founder’s ambition is for the practice to become a trusted point of reference in Kenya and across Africa for legal matters shaped by technology.

What guides us

Values that can be seen in the work.

The firm’s values are practical commitments. They influence how we prepare, communicate and take responsibility for the work entrusted to us.

01

Professionalism

We approach each instruction with preparation, courtesy and attention to the quality of the work.

02

Integrity

We value honest advice, ethical conduct and confidentiality in every professional relationship.

03

Accountability

We take responsibility for our role, communicate clearly and use time and resources with care.

04

Justice with fairness

We believe that firm legal representation and fair treatment should remain closely connected.

Our approach

Clear thinking before action.

Legal work is most useful when the client understands the reasoning behind the next step.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We begin with the client’s account, immediate concerns and desired outcome.

  2. 02

    Understand

    We examine the relevant facts, documents, digital information and legal context.

  3. 03

    Clarify

    We explain the available options, likely implications and issues that require attention.

  4. 04

    Act

    We agree on a practical course and support the client through the work that follows.

Community & mentorship

Knowledge can help young people choose a better path.

The firm’s outlook extends beyond client matters to mentorship and the development of practical skills among young people.

By supporting confidence, responsible decision-making and useful soft skills, the firm hopes to contribute to communities in which young people are better equipped to avoid crime and harmful behaviour.
