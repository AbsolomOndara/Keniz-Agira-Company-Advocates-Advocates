The legal scope

Personal information creates responsibilities for organisations and rights for the people whose data is collected or used.

We help clients understand privacy obligations, review documentation and decide how to respond to concerns involving personal information.

The precise scope of work, jurisdiction and any need for external technical expertise will be confirmed before the firm accepts an instruction.
  • 01Privacy notices and internal policies
  • 02Lawful collection and use of personal data
  • 03Data subject requests and complaints
  • 04Vendor and data-processing arrangements
  • 05Data breach assessment and notification
  • 06Cross-border data considerations

Starting carefully

What happens at the beginning?

A useful first discussion focuses on what happened, when it happened, which people or systems are involved and what information is currently available.

  1. Describe the immediate concern without altering potential evidence.
  2. Identify relevant dates, accounts, devices, records and organisations.
  3. Clarify the legal question and any time-sensitive obligations.
  4. Agree on scope, next steps and any independent expertise required.
