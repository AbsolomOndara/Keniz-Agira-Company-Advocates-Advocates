Digital Evidence & Forensics

Legal guidance for clients in Kenya, provided within the firm’s confirmed scope and applicable jurisdiction.

AboutHow we helpInsights

Digital material can be valuable evidence, but its usefulness depends on how it is identified, preserved, explained and presented. Poor handling can weaken an otherwise important record.

The firm provides legal guidance on electronic evidence and works with appropriately qualified forensic specialists where technical examination is required. The legal and technical roles remain clearly defined.

How we help

  • Legal assessment of electronic and digital material
  • Evidence preservation and chain-of-custody guidance
  • Admissibility and presentation considerations
  • Instructions to independent forensic specialists
  • Review of digital evidence in disputes and investigations
  • Guidance concerning devices, messages, records and platform data
